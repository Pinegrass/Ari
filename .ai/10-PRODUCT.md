# Product
Ari is a personal-finance app with transactions, budgets, goals, reports, tax tools, bills, notes and Tomo AI coaching. Primary users are individuals managing everyday money across mobile and web. The promise is a clear localised financial picture plus bounded coaching based on user data. Major workflows include secure auth, transaction/budget/goal management, reports, accountant tools, coaching, subscription and data rights. Mobile is the parent repository’s primary target; web and Flask backend are nested repositories. It is not a bank, broker, tax-filing service or fiduciary adviser.
